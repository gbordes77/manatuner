High-Level Data Flow

Understanding how ManaTuner processes your decklists in real-time, client-side.

1

Deck Parser & Scryfall Enrichment

Decklists are parsed locally. Card types, mana symbols, and produced mana are loaded and cached via Bounded LRU cache.

2

Mana Acceleration & Land Analyzer

Models complex lands (ETB tapped conditions) and 13 ramp behaviors (dorks, rocks, doublers) dynamically based on the current deck context.

3

Hypergeometric & Monte Carlo Engines

Runs dynamic hypergeometric distributions for base odds, and delegates 10,000+ hand simulations to Web Workers for mulligan calculations.

4

Redux Store & Interactive Dashboard

Calculated probabilities are saved into the Redux state to render responsive charts, curves, and actionable land Recommendations.
